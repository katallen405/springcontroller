Start the gen3 controller node:
ros2 run gen3_torque_control gen3_torque_node --ros-args -r joint_states:=/kinova/joint_states_lowlevel

Start the springcontroller (any config works here -- the workspace
calibration step below adds/removes its own demo spring on top of
whatever's loaded):
ros2 launch springcontroller gen3_spring.launch.py armviz:=true load_collision_scene:=true config:=/home/katallen/sandbox/src/springcontroller/springcontroller/config/gen3_springs.yaml

Start the webserver:
ros2 launch springcontroller_ui study_control_panel.launch.py

Open the study controller webserver:
http://localhost:8090/

--- Per-participant calibration (once, before their 3 randomized-order runs) ---

1. Move the arm to the study start location ("Study start location" panel).
2. Seat the participant so their midline is at the pre-set seat location.
3. Adjust the chair height so their elbows are at table height.
4. Measure seated eye height (meter stick, from the table surface) and
   forearm length elbow-to-fingertips (tape laid out on the table), both in
   centimeters -- that's what the panel expects.
5. In the "Study workspace calibration" panel: enter the participant ID and
   both measurements in cm, click "Preview workspace center" (places a live
   0-deadband demo spring -- enable torque control to see/feel it).
6. Push the arm around and/or retarget "workspace_demo" in the "Add /
   remove springs" list below until the location is approved.
7. Click "Finalize study conditions" -- writes
   ~/gen3_study_data/<participant ID>/condition1.yaml and condition2.yaml,
   logs the session to ~/gen3_study_data/measurements.csv, and removes the
   demo spring.

--- The participant's 3 conditions, in whatever random order they draw ---

Baseline (no torque controller):
  Don't enable torque control for this condition -- gravity comp/springs
  never engage. (Or relaunch step 2 above with
  config:=.../springcontroller/config/gen3_springs_none.yaml if the panel
  is currently pointed at a different config.)

Condition 1 (relaunch step 2 above with):
  config:=~/gen3_study_data/<participant ID>/condition1.yaml

Condition 2 (relaunch step 2 above with):
  config:=~/gen3_study_data/<participant ID>/condition2.yaml
